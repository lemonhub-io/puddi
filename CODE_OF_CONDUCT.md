# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want the Puddi community to be welcoming and free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Be patient with newcomers; everyone was new once.
- Give and accept constructive, technical criticism graciously. Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification before assuming bad intent.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologize to those affected and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Insulting or derogatory comments, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, including continued contact after being asked to stop.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: the repositories of the [puddi-lang](https://github.com/puddi-lang) organization, their issues, pull requests and discussions, and any other channel run by the project. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainers by opening a private report through the **Report a vulnerability** button on the [Security tab](https://github.com/puddi-lang/puddi/security) of the puddi repository, with "Code of conduct" in the title. GitHub shows such reports only to the maintainers. Until the project has a dedicated contact address this is the private channel for conduct reports as well as security issues.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the reporter. A maintainer involved in a report takes no part in handling it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide on the response to a report, guided by these steps:

1. **Correction.** A private, written explanation of what was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behavior, and no interaction with the people involved for a period of time.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from interaction with the project, for a pattern of violations or a severe single one.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the code of conduct in good faith may face consequences determined by the other maintainers.
